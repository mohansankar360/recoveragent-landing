If you run a Shopify store in India with COD, return-to-origin (RTO) is rarely a single problem. It is the bill you pay when unverified orders ship, carts go cold, and failed deliveries never get a second chance.

This guide maps the leaks Recover Agent is built to address — without promising a magic percentage. Your baseline depends on category, city mix, and how you confirm orders today.

## What RTO actually costs you

Every RTO order burns freight twice, ties up working capital, and distorts your realisation rate. Shopify revenue and bank deposits diverge because returned parcels never convert to cash.

Use the [loss calculator](/loss-calculator) with your COD share, average order value, and order volume to estimate monthly leakage. That number is the starting point — not an industry average.

## The three leaks most Indian D2C brands share

### 1. Unverified COD before dispatch

Orders ship without a confirmation that the buyer still wants the product at that address. Fake, duplicate, or impulse COD orders become RTOs in transit.

Recover Agent calls customers before dispatch to confirm intent — see [COD verification](/cod-verification) and the [control room](/control-room) workflow.

### 2. Abandoned checkouts

Shoppers add to cart but never pay. Email and passive WhatsApp nudges often arrive too late.

Voice outreach brings the conversation forward while intent is still warm. See [abandoned checkout recovery](/abandoned-checkout-recovery).

### 3. Failed deliveries (NDR)

When the courier marks a non-delivery report, many brands rely on the carrier's default re-attempt window. If the customer is not reached, the order returns.

Recover Agent calls after a failed delivery to reschedule or resolve the issue — [NDR recovery](/ndr-recovery).

## What to fix before you add another tool

1. **Measure your baseline** — RTO rate on COD orders only, not blended with prepaid.
2. **Confirm before ship** — even a manual sample proves which orders would have returned.
3. **Close the loop on NDR** — know how many failed deliveries get a customer conversation before RTO.
4. **Track outcomes in one place** — not across spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads.
