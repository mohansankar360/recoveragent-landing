Cash-on-delivery is still the default for much of Indian ecommerce. It also creates a verification gap: the order exists in Shopify, but you do not know if the customer still wants it until the courier knocks — or until the parcel comes back as RTO.

COD verification before dispatch closes that gap.

## What COD verification means

Before you hand the order to your 3PL, you confirm:

- The customer placed the order intentionally
- The delivery address is correct
- The customer is available to receive the parcel

That is different from a marketing WhatsApp blast. It is a confirmation step tied to a specific order about to ship.

## Why SMS and WhatsApp alone fall short

Messages can be ignored, buried in promotions, or read hours later. By then the order may already be in the manifest.

## What a good confirmation call covers

A useful COD confirmation is short and transactional:

1. Greet the customer in their language
2. Reference the order and amount
3. Confirm address and availability
4. Capture intent — confirm, reschedule, or cancel before shipping
